The Ecosystem

Six modules. One connected
coffee intelligence layer.

Each module feeds into the next, creating a self-reinforcing cycle of data, assets, and value — from the roastery floor to the global marketplace.

Curve IP Database

The world's largest repository of professional roast curves. 10,000+ cataloged profiles, targeting 1M by 2027. Every curve is a licensable digital asset.

AI Roasting Hardware

Smart roasters that execute optimal curves autonomously. Consistent quality, batch after batch. 100+ units deployed, scaling to 1,000 by 2027.

Roaster Alliance

A global community of professional roasters sharing techniques, curves, and market access. 500+ members today, growing to 10,000.

RWA Digital Passport

Blockchain-verified digital identity for every batch. Provenance, quality metrics, and ownership — immutable and transparent. 10,000+ issued.

NFT Marketplace

Trade roast curves, digital collectibles, and coffee IP assets in a purpose-built marketplace. Royalties flow back to creators automatically.

Supply Chain Finance

Blockchain-powered trade finance for the coffee value chain. Faster settlements, lower costs, and full transparency from farm to cup.
